How it works

Four steps. Each one gives you something you can use, even if you stop there.

  1. 1

    Upload your CV

    A PDF or Word file is enough. Answer a few questions about the work you want.

  2. 2

    See your offer and rate

    What you could sell, to whom, and what people in your field charge. Based on official Dutch income figures and rate surveys.

  3. 3

    Find who might hire you

    Companies that fit your offer, freelance jobs on platforms, and other freelancers who share work.

  4. 4

    Pitch, and learn from every reply

    We draft your pitches. You approve them. After each job, client feedback shapes your next offer.

You're in control. The AI does the legwork.

Our AI suggests and drafts. It doesn't judge you, and it never sends an email in your name without your OK.

  • You approve every direct email. Drafts wait for you to read, change and send.
  • Auto-apply is off until you switch it on. Use it only for freelance platforms you choose.
  • We show our sources. Every rate comes with where it came from, so you can check it. See our sources.
  • Dutch rules built in. Offers are written so you work as a real independent, not a hidden employee.

Not every pitch lands. We help you keep going.

Some pitches will get no reply. That's normal, and it isn't a verdict on you. We help you learn what works.

Feedback on what you do well
