The gap between what clients offer and what freelancers ask is real. Chipi shows what clients offer, because that's what you'll be negotiating against.

How we build your range

  1. Match your field. We map the work on your CV to the closest field in the rate data (for example, project management).
  2. Start from what clients offer. The field's average offered rate is the middle of your range.
  3. Adjust for experience. Starters usually begin lower; people with many years and a clear speciality can usually ask more. We widen or shift the range accordingly, and say so.
  4. Check it against reality. As you pitch, real replies and accepted quotes show whether the range holds. Chipi suggests a change only once there's enough evidence, and asks you first.
  5. Never below your floor. You set a minimum. Chipi never suggests or quotes below it.

Every rate in Chipi links back to the source it came from, so you can check it yourself.

What the numbers can't tell you

  • Averages hide spread. Two people in the same field can earn very different rates.
  • Platforms aren't the whole market. Rates offered on a platform can differ from direct clients and agencies.
  • An hourly rate isn't income. Unbilled hours, holidays, insurance, pension and taxes all come out of it.
  • Figures age. Markets move. Each number shows the period it describes.
  • This isn't financial or tax advice. For your own situation, talk to an accountant or adviser.
