The short version

  • We never sell your data, not to advertisers, recruiters or anyone else.
  • Only you see your results. A company sees only a message you chose to send it.
  • Nothing is sent in your name without your OK.
  • Delete everything in one click. Your CV and profile are removed right away; backups clear within 30 days.
  • Your data is not used to train AI models.

What we collect

Data you give us

  • Your CV and anything in it: name, contact details, work history, education, skills.
  • Your answers about the work you want: hours, income goal, the work you'd rather avoid, where you want to work.
  • Your decisions: which leads you mark useful or skip, the drafts you edit, approve or reject, and any notes you add.
  • Messages you send to us, for example through the contact page.

Data created while you use Chipi

  • Your profile and results: the services, rate range, offers and leads Chipi prepares for you.
  • Outreach history: drafts, what was sent, replies you receive and the outcome.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and timestamps in our server logs, used to keep the service running and secure.

We don't ask for special categories of data (such as health, religion or ethnicity). If your CV contains any, you can remove it before uploading; we don't use it.

AI and your data

Chipi uses AI models to read your CV, suggest services, estimate a rate range, rank opportunities and draft messages. How that works is explained in plain words on How we use AI. For your data, it means:

  • No automated decisions about you. AI output is a suggestion. Nothing with legal or similarly significant effect happens without you deciding (GDPR art. 22).
  • Not used to train models. We send data to our AI provider through its business API, which by default does not use it to train or improve its models.
  • Limited retention by the provider. The AI provider may keep requests for up to 30 days to detect abuse, unless the law requires longer, and then deletes them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • See the data we hold about you (access).
  • Correct anything that's wrong (rectification).
  • Delete your data (erasure). The "Delete everything" button does this instantly.
  • Download your data in a common format (portability).
  • Limit or object to how we use it (restriction and objection).
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

Email [privacy@yourdomain.nl] to use any of these rights. We reply within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you're not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

Security

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Access to production data is limited to the people who need it to run the service.
  • Sending in your name requires your approval, enforced in code, not only in policy.
  • If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we tell you and the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens within 72 hours of finding out.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell analytics data. The app keeps only what it needs to work while you use it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we update this page and the date at the top. For important changes we email you before they take effect.
